# AI Usage

Digital Governance Paper Notes is maintained with assistance from AI and large language model systems. That assistance is part of the repository's production method and is disclosed here so readers can distinguish computational assistance from editorial authority.

AI/LLM systems may assist with paper ingestion, source extraction, metadata enrichment, comparative retrieval, candidate relationship discovery, governance-facet suggestions, synthesis drafting, version comparison, repository maintenance, issue and pull-request preparation, and generation of discovery surfaces. The **Archive as Knowledge Infrastructure** programme explicitly uses these capabilities to make relationships, provenance, recurring governance questions, and cumulative findings more discoverable across the archive.

AI assistance does not make an AI system an author, reviewer of record, or source of legitimacy. The human maintainer remains responsible for accepting or rejecting analytical claims, governance interpretations, relationship edges, facet assignments, synthesis conclusions, corrections, and publication decisions. In particular, judgments about authority, legitimacy, accountability, control, institutional implications, novelty, enforcement, revocation, redress, and contestability remain editorial judgments rather than machine determinations.

## Evidence and inference

Factual claims should remain grounded in the reviewed paper, stable publication metadata, or repository evidence. AI-generated extraction or inference is not treated as evidence merely because it is plausible. Where an AI system proposes a relationship, classification, interpretation, or synthesis claim, that proposal must be checked against the underlying source material before it becomes canonical repository content.

The repository does not claim that AI-assisted synthesis is neutral, exhaustive, or free of model error. Derived outputs may omit relevant work, overstate similarity, miss disagreement, or reproduce biases present in models or source material. They are therefore reviewable editorial artifacts and may be corrected, rejected, revised, or superseded through normal repository history.

## Canonical and generated content

Human-authored or human-accepted Markdown, YAML and JSON files in the repository are canonical source material. Generated Pages artifacts are discovery surfaces, not independent sources of truth. AI-assisted enrichment must not silently overwrite canonical review prose. Machine-generated suggestions become canonical only through an explicit repository change that can be reviewed in git history.

## Privacy and source availability

Private, confidential, or otherwise non-public source material should not be represented as publicly verifiable evidence unless it is actually made available through an appropriate public citation or repository artifact. AI assistance does not change the evidentiary status of a source.

## Scope of disclosure

This policy applies to individual paper reviews, repository maintenance, collection synthesis, provenance enrichment, relationship modelling, governance facets, correction and supersession analysis, and other Archive as Knowledge Infrastructure outputs. The degree of AI assistance may vary by artifact. The common rule is that assistance is disclosed, source grounding remains necessary, and human editorial responsibility remains visible.
